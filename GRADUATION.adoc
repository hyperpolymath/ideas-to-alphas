= Graduation rule — katagoria is an incubator, not a family
:revdate: 2026-10-12

An idea may live here as a sketch, a prototype, or a prover-agnostic
scribble. It *leaves* only when a family repo (echo, choreographic, tropical,
epistemic, occupancy, residual-evidence, secret) holds its core theorem
kernel-checked under the family ruling (Agda `--safe --without-K`) with the
six-item kit: a theorem a reader could bet against, a separation witness
from its nearest sibling, reject controls, a laws census, one sibling
connection discharged by import, and a PROOF-STATUS where prose and
artefact agree. Until then its status here is DESIGNED at best, and this
file is the only place that may say so.
